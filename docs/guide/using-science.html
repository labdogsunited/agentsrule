Science panels

The Science desktop holds panels for asking what a run did. They share a few rules:

  • Every answer carries a stamp: the world, the tick, the revision and the backend it was read at. An answer for a past interval says what scope it really has (the engine cannot re-observe a tick that has passed).
  • Reading never changes the run. Asking and not asking give the same world, tick for tick.
  • One selection across panels. Brush a span of ticks on Lineage History, or select a lineage or a creature, and Life Histories, the census, Ancestry, the Budget and the Flow Map follow it, or say they cannot.
  • A blank is not a zero. Where a panel could not find something, it says "none found", and where the backend cannot answer it says why.

The panels

PanelQuestion it answers
Rule Lenswhat runs in a tick, in what order? Modes: *Lens* (the checked schedule), *Matrix* (which statements read or write which declaration), *Activity* (what each statement did), *Trace* (a bounded recording of transfer requests)
Lineage Historyhow did each lineage's population change?
Genome Censuswhat do the living carry? Active genes by lineage, a comparison of two creatures, and where each gene is carried
Resource Budgetwhere did the upkeep substance come from and go? Stock then and now, every line with its sign, and an *unreconciled* amount that is shown and never forced to zero
Ancestrywho are this creature's ancestors and descendants?
Life Historieshow old are the living and what do they carry? How did a birth cohort fare?
Regionwhat is in this rectangle of the world, in one call?
Flow Mapwhich reactions ran, where, and what blocked them?
Compare Runsdo two worlds differ, and could the setup explain it? Identical setups are said to be repeated trials of one question
Notebookbookmarks that copy their evidence, edits and their timing, exportable as JSON

What the GPU can answer

Reading a budget

The budget of a lineage closes: its stock changed by what its creatures made, used, took, stole, gave, dropped and lost, minus upkeep and fees. If the lines do not explain the change, the difference is shown as unreconciled. Stock that creatures bring in by genesis or injection after the start is not counted, and shows up there.
